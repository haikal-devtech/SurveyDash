# Motion and reused artwork

The user clarified that the existing generated pictures should be used again. The original small transparent WebP assets are reused: the hero appears on the public role-entry page and manager workspace, share art appears beside the surveyor greeting, and invite art appears in the surveyor's empty-task state. They remain visible in compact mobile compositions. Existing create/results/help illustrations are retained. No new image generation, raster editing, video downloads or animation packages were needed.

The Instagram account supplied as MotionDesignHQ could not be inspected: the public profile fetch was throttled, and exact-name searches returned no matching public reels. No videos from that account were watched, and this implementation does not claim to reproduce one. Accessible primary references were [Motion Primitives](https://github.com/ibelick/motion-primitives), [Motion's animation guide](https://motion.dev/docs/react-animation), [MDN Element.animate](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Element/animate) and [Radix animations](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/guides/animation). Original native implementation uses their general patterns of selection continuity, brief arrivals and state feedback; no reference application code or assets were copied.

The sidebar, survey editor and surveyor navigation share a measured moving selection surface. Semantic links/buttons and their current state remain intact; without measurement the selected control keeps its existing fallback style. Measurements happen on selection/resize, not every animation frame. The existing explorer selection line and position-only list transitions are preserved.

Marked dashboard surfaces arrive once per route/panel change through cancellable Web Animations. Reads are batched, nested scenes do not animate the same surface twice, only visible surfaces are considered, and at most 12 surfaces animate. Stagger is capped at 132ms on desktop and 72ms on mobile; total arrival time stays below 500ms/350ms respectively. Typing and account/data refresh within a scene do not replay it. Content is visible in SSR and remains usable throughout. No form or route content is retained or remounted to wait for an exit animation. The existing field state and tutorial host are preserved.

Real dashboard chart shapes have a finite entrance: a drawn trend line, faded area, rising columns, horizontal bar growth and a small settling round chart. Labels, accessible descriptions and totals render their real values immediately. There is no fake count-up or interpolated response data. Motion is opt-in through the authenticated dashboard provider. Slide/export chart variants remain static, public/respondent charts do not opt in, CSS print rendering removes chart/art animation, and scene arrivals cancel before print. Existing role, privacy, ownership and submission boundaries are unchanged.

OS reduced-motion preference removes spatial scene/chart/art/pill animation. Scene/list animations cancel on preference changes, resize, hidden document and unmount. A settled event lets the tutorial remeasure its spotlight after native animation finishes; CSS animation completion already had its own guide listener. Visual timing, frame rate, mobile cropping, pointer/focus and actual walkthrough execution still require browser/device QA. The saved automatic browser-approval policy prevents that here; SSR, unit and HTTP checks are not a substitute.
