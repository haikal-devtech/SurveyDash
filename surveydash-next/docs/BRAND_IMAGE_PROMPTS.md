# Historical SurveyDash photography prompts — superseded

This first photography direction was replaced at the user's request by Soft Claymorphism + Gradient Design. The current production assets and prompts are documented in `CLAY_IMAGE_PROMPTS.md`. The obsolete photos are removed from public delivery. Their original PNGs remain in the local workspace at `.data/brand-source/` for reference. The historical prompts below were executed with the built-in ImageGen tool on 20 October 2026 and do not describe real staff, customers or respondents.

Images were inspected for content, brand palette, visible text, and anatomy. File export uses Sharp for resizing and WebP encoding. Layout verification in a browser remains deferred.

## hero.webp

Create one premium photorealistic editorial photograph for the Indonesian survey platform SurveyDash Studio website. Landscape 3:2 composition. Two young adult Indonesian researchers, a woman in a soft lavender blouse and a man in a charcoal shirt, thoughtfully reviewing anonymous survey results together on a laptop at a bright contemporary workspace. Warm natural daylight, cream walls, subtle purple stationery, realistic friendly candid expression, understated professional style, photographic skin texture, realistic anatomy and hands. Spacious calm composition, subjects on the right with soft blurred lavender foreground on left. Violet and lavender brand palette, dark navy details. No text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no recognizable software interface, no fake charts or claims. Single photograph, not a collage. Intended use: landing page visual.
